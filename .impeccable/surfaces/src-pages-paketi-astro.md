# Inner pages: Paketi, Akademija, O nama, Kontakt

Scope: the four pages below home, both languages. Mode: Persuade (Kontakt leans Operate: the task
is to reach the team). Each page keeps its search intent (title, meta, h1, headings); the redesign
changes composition, not targeting. World: inherited from the home page (ink ground, Bricolage
display, teal accent, gold for the morning and the gold price, real product UI as imagery).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each page is led by the one real artifact that proves its claim, placed beside a
left-aligned headline, instead of the shared centred hero with a curved horizon and floating
cards. Refuses: centred template heroes, identical rounded cards, tabbed content that hides text,
stock photos, accent-coloured words.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ground, hairline rules instead of card shells, the home's gold-to-teal day line as
the one structural colour (a straight baseline under every hero, the Academy rail, the journey
rail), Bricolage for titles and big figures, Plex Sans for reading, mono only for real data.

STORY: Plans: see the three plans as one ladder, compare, buy. Academy: see the whole syllabus
from zero to pro, how a module works, start free. About: why we exist, the risk chart, principles
and path in full. Contact: pick a channel in the first viewport, or write; the page ends on support details, with no closing call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: crumbs, h1 (Bricolage, ~72px), lead, page action. Right/below: the
page's artifact (billing + guarantee, the level staircase, the risk chart, the channel list).
A straight gold-to-teal hairline closes the hero band.
